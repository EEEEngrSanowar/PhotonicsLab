Photonic Neuromorphic Computing

Photonic neuromorphic computing enables ultra-fast, energy-efficient AI hardware for defense signal processing — terahertz bandwidths, sub-nanosecond latency, and EM immunity beyond electronic reach. Our work spans the architecture, device building blocks, and foundry-compatible implementation of these systems, with a focus on manufacturable circuits on standard silicon photonics platforms.
