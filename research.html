On-Chip Photonic AI Accelerator

We design on-chip photonic AI accelerators — neuromorphic and reservoir computing architectures on standard silicon photonics platforms — for ultra-fast, energy-efficient inference. Operating at 1550 nm with terahertz bandwidths, sub-nanosecond latency, and EM immunity, these circuits enable non-von Neumann processing of time-series, speech, and defense signals on a single manufacturable chip, spanning the architecture, device building blocks, and foundry-compatible implementation.
